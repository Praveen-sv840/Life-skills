# Grit and Growth Mindset

### Question 1. Paraphrase the Grit video.

Grit means having passion and continuously working towards our goals without giving up. Talent alone is not enough to achieve success; hard work, patience and consistency also matter.

### Question 2. Introduction to Growth Mindset

A growth mindset means believing that we can improve our skills and knowledge through practice and effort. Instead of being afraid of mistakes or failures, we should take them as opportunities to learn something new.

### Question 3. What is Internal Locus of Control? What is the key point in the video?

Internal Locus of Control means believing that our actions and decisions play an important role in our success or failure. The main point of the video is that instead of blaming situations or other people, we should take responsibility for our actions and focus on what we can improve.

### Question 4. What are the key points mentioned by the speaker to build a Growth Mindset?

1. Accept challenges.
2. Don't be afraid of making mistakes.
3. Learn from failures.
4. Put effort into learning new things.
5. Accept feedback and work on it.
6. Try different approaches to solve problems.
7. Come out of your comfort zone.
8. Be patient and consistent.
9. Believe in yourself and your ability to improve.

### Question 5. What are your ideas to take action and build a Growth Mindset?

As a software developer, I want to improve myself every day by learning new concepts and practising coding regularly. Whenever I face errors or get stuck while solving a problem, instead of giving up, I will try to understand the reason and find a solution. I will also take feedback from my mentors and teammates, work on my weak areas and try to learn from their experience. I believe that consistency and being open to learning will help me become a better developer.
