### Question 1
**What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.**

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we understand a concept deeply by explaining it in simple words as if we are teaching someone else.

### Question 3
**What are active and diffused modes of thinking?**

- **Active (Focused) Mode:** Concentrating deeply on a particular task or problem without distractions.
- **Diffused Mode:** Relaxing the mind and allowing it to make connections between different ideas, which can help us understand difficult concepts.

### Question 4
**According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic? Only mention the points.**

1. Choose what you want to learn.
2. Focus on one skill at a time.
3. Break the topic into smaller parts.
4. Identify the most important concepts.
5. Remove distractions from your learning environment.
6. Spend at least 20 hours practising.
7. Learn by doing and correct your mistakes.
8. Practise consistently.
